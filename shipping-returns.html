Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

Where We Ship

We currently ship to all 50 U.S. states. International shipping is not available at this time.

Shipping Rates

Orders $50+: Free standard shipping
Orders under $50: Flat rate $5.99

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

Delivery Time

Standard shipping typically arrives within 5–8 business days after processing. Delivery times may vary based on your location and carrier conditions.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this to follow your shipment's progress.

Shipping Issues

If your package is lost, damaged, or significantly delayed, contact us at info@drinkwanderstate.com and we'll help resolve it.

Returns & Refunds

Our Promise

We want you to love every sip of Wander State. If something isn't right — a damaged shipment, a quality issue, or you're just not feeling it — reach out and we'll make it right.

Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're not satisfied with your order, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll work with you on a replacement or refund. No fine print, no runaround.

Damaged or Defective Orders

If your order arrives damaged or defective, email us at info@drinkwanderstate.com with a photo of the issue. We'll send a replacement at no cost.

How to Reach Us

  • Email: info@drinkwanderstate.com
  • Subject line: Order Issue — [your order number]
  • Response time: We'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and returned to your original payment method. You'll receive a confirmation email when your refund is issued.
