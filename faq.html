About Our Products

Wander State is a line of functional sparkling teas crafted with real tea, botanicals, pure maple water, and a blend of three adaptogenic mushrooms: Lion's Mane, Reishi, and Cordyceps.

We created Wander State for people who want to feel good without the jitters, crashes, or artificial ingredients found in most energy drinks and sodas.

We offer four unique flavors, each designed for different moments:

  • Lemon & Mint — Crisp and energizing, perfect for morning clarity
  • Chamomile & Blackberry — Soothing and calming, ideal for evening relaxation
  • Yuzu & Cucumber — Refreshing and light, great for movement and endurance
  • Hibiscus & Blood Orange — Vibrant and festive, made for social moments

Zero added sugar. Our teas are naturally sweetened with pure maple water, which provides a subtle, clean sweetness without the blood sugar spike. Each can has approximately 4-6 grams of naturally occurring sugars from the maple water and fruit essences.

Yes. All our teas and botanicals are certified organic. Our mushroom extracts are grown using organic practices at Monterey Nutra's facilities in California. The maple water comes from sustainably managed forests in the Northeast.

Each 12oz can contains approximately 20-30 calories, depending on the flavor. This makes Wander State a light, refreshing alternative to sodas, juices, and traditional energy drinks.

About Functional Mushrooms

Each mushroom in our blend serves a specific purpose:

  • Lion's Mane — Supports cognitive function, memory, and mental clarity
  • Reishi — Known as the "mushroom of immortality," supports stress relief and immune function

Together, they create a balanced, full-spectrum blend that supports both mind and body.

All our mushrooms are grown and processed by Monterey Nutra in Watsonville, California. They use a dual-extraction process (hot water + alcohol) to capture both water-soluble and fat-soluble compounds, resulting in a more complete, bioavailable extract.

Every batch is third-party tested for purity, potency, and contaminants. We publish Certificates of Analysis for full transparency.

Dietary & Allergens

Yes. All Wander State products are 100% plant-based and vegan-friendly.

Yes. Our teas are naturally gluten-free. Our facility and suppliers follow strict protocols to prevent cross-contamination.

Some flavors contain naturally occurring caffeine from real tea:

  • Lemon & Mint — Contains green tea (~25-30mg caffeine, about 1/3 of a cup of coffee)
  • Yuzu & Cucumber — Contains white tea (~15-20mg caffeine)
  • Chamomile & Blackberry — Caffeine-free (herbal tea base)
  • Hibiscus & Blood Orange — Caffeine-free (herbal tea base)

Wander State is free from the top 8 allergens (milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, and soy). However, our products are made in facilities that may process other ingredients. If you have severe allergies, please contact us for detailed ingredient and facility information.
