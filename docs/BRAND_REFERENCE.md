# Wander State Brand Reference

> **Purpose:** Quick reference for all brand guidelines, voice, and creative direction.
> **Last Updated:** February 6, 2026

---

## Brand Overview

**Company:** Wander State
**Product:** Functional Sparkling Mushroom Tea
**Tagline:** Nourish Your Journey
**Mission:** Redefine transparency in functional beverages while helping people reconnect with themselves, each other, and nature.

**Brand Personality:** Natural, transparent, wellness-focused, adventurous, premium yet approachable

---

## Brand Pillars

1. **Authenticity & Integrity** — Honest sourcing, transparency, environmental responsibility
2. **Bridging Wellness & Nature** — Premium ingredients from sustainable farms/forests
3. **Exploration & Curiosity** — Spirit of adventure, openness to new experiences
4. **Connection** — To self, others, and the natural world

---

## Target Audience

- Professionals, athletes, wellness enthusiasts (ages 25-45)
- Urban millennials and Gen Z
- Sober-curious individuals
- Health-conscious, nature-connected people
- Open-minded individuals seeking deeper connections to nature and themselves

## Key Differentiators (Storytelling-Focused)

- **Farmer partnerships you can be proud of** — Real people, named places, sustainable practices
- **Patience over shortcuts** — 100+ day mushroom extraction, trees that stand for generations
- **Land that's protected forever** — Conservation easements, bird-friendly forests, regenerative practices
- **Community connections** — Jamaican seasonal workers, local teams, multigenerational farming
- **Ingredients with provenance** — San Diego to the Adirondacks, you can trace every sip
- **Maple water sweetened** — No added sugar, natural electrolytes
- **Organic and Non-GMO**

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- Warm and inviting, not clinical
- Curious and adventurous, not preachy
- Transparent and honest, not salesy
- Nature-connected, not granola-crunchy
- Premium but approachable

### Writing Style
- Lead with stories, not statistics
- Celebrate people and places, not lab results
- Use "we" and "our" to feel inclusive
- Active voice, conversational
- Short sentences, scannable content

### Words We Use
- Nourish, journey, wander, explore, connect
- Curious, intentional, mindful, present
- Craft, cultivate, source, partner
- Clarity, calm, energy, balance

### Words We Avoid
- Clinical/scientific jargon (bioavailability, COA, etc.)
- Overpromising health claims
- Corporate speak
- "Clean" (overused in wellness)
